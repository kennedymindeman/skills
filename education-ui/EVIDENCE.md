# Evidence behind education-ui

Each principle in `SKILL.md` maps to a finding below. Full review with DOIs, provenance grades, and the folklore list: `~/projects/learn/docs/research/ui-design-evidence/README.md`. The line-by-line vetting of Anthropic's `frontend-design` skill that produced this variant: `~/projects/learn/docs/research/design-skills/vetting-frontend-design.md`.

| Principle | Finding | Source |
|---|---|---|
| First screen is the task; prototypical layout | Appeal judged in 50 ms; low visual complexity and prototypicality are the main predictors | Lindgaard 2006; Tuch 2012; Reinecke 2013 |
| Subject flavour without decoration | Seductive details g≈−0.16 on comprehension, recall, transfer | Sundararajan & Adesope 2020; Harp & Mayer 1998 |
| One type family; ≥16-18px; 55-75 cpl; spacing | Serif/sans negligible on screen; size and spacing help all readers | Bernard 2003; Dyson 2004; Rello & Baeza-Yates 2016 |
| Positive polarity default | Dark-on-light gives better acuity and proofreading at all ages | Piepenbrock 2013, 2014 |
| Contrast 4.5:1 / 3:1 | WCAG 1.4.3 derivation from Arditi & Faye | W3C |
| Structure is information; signal sparingly | Signaling g≈0.38, largest for low prior knowledge | Richter 2016; Noetel 2022 |
| Redundant encoding, neutral error colour | ~8% CVD in men; red-impairment effect contested | Wong 2011; Elliot 2007 and failed replications |
| Adjacent, segmented, disclosed | Spatial contiguity d≈0.72; segmentation g≈0.32-0.36; expertise reversal | Ginns 2006; Noetel 2022; Kalyuga |
| Elaborated, immediate, task-level feedback | Elaborated 0.49 > correct-answer 0.32 > right/wrong 0.05; praise least effective; delay hurts | Van der Kleij 2015; Hattie & Timperley 2007; Kluger & DeNisi 1996 |
| Autonomy-supportive, no dark patterns | Autonomy support ≈0.32 on motivation/engagement; dark-pattern taxonomy and prevalence | Ryan & Deci 2020; Gray 2018; Mathur 2019 |
| Motion shows change only | Animation ≤ equivalent static graphic; helps only when depicting motion/process | Tversky 2002; Höffler & Leutner 2007; WCAG 2.3.3 |
| Moderate density; large near targets | Fitts robust in UI; Hick weak for searchable menus | Fitts 1954; Cockburn 2007 |
| Judge by task success | Aesthetic-usability effect is correlational and bidirectional | Kurosu & Kashimura 1995; Tuch 2012 |
