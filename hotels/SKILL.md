# Hotels

Link each hotel to its own booking page, with dates and guest count filled in when known, next to its Google Maps link (`https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=<name and address, URL-encoded>`).

Use a third-party booking site only when the hotel's own page is unavailable, and say so.
